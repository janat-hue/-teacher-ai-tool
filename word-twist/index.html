What is an anagram?

ENGLISH

An anagram is a word or phrase made by rearranging all the letters of another word or phrase. Use each letter exactly once for each time it appears.

ČESKY

Anagram neboli přesmyčka je slovo nebo slovní spojení, které vznikne přeskupením všech písmen jiného výrazu. Každé písmeno použij tolikrát, kolikrát se v původním výrazu vyskytuje.

УКРАЇНСЬКОЮ

Анаграма — це слово або словосполучення, утворене перестановкою всіх літер іншого слова чи словосполучення. Кожну літеру потрібно використати стільки разів, скільки вона зустрічається в початковому слові.

↻Same letters, same number of letters.You may change their order, but you may not add or remove any.
MORE THAN A WORD PUZZLE

Same letters. New possibilities.

Word Twist combines English practice with the pleasure of discovery. Retrieve words you know, uncover new ones and notice how a different order of letters creates a different meaning.

What can you practise?

Vocabulary & spelling

Look closely at how words are built. Meaning clues connect the written word with what it means.

Flexible thinking

Try combinations, look for patterns and change your approach when an idea does not work.

Attention & checking

Keep track of the letters you use and check your solution against the rules.

Independence & persistence

Try again, choose a hint or move on. A mistake can be the start of another attempt.

Motivation & confidence

Short challenges, immediate feedback and a choice of difficulty make progress visible. There is no timer.

Collaboration & communication

Play with a partner: explain your ideas, compare strategies and learn from each other.
